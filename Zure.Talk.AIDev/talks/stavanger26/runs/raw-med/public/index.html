    Arena

      • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, one cell per press (max 5 moves a second).
      • Press Space to strike: 25 damage to everyone in the 8 cells around you.
      • After a strike you wait 1 second before you can strike again.
      • Everyone starts with 100 HP. At 0 HP you are out, but you can keep watching.
      • The safe zone shrinks by one cell on every side every 15 seconds, down to 4 by 4.
      • Outside the zone you lose 10 HP every second.
      • Last one standing wins. If the last players fall together, nobody wins.
      • Points: 100 per kill, 10 for each zone shrink you survive, 250 for winning.
      • Alone in the arena? A bot joins your round. Joining mid-round? You play the next one.
      • Lost connection? You have 10 seconds to come back to keep your place.
