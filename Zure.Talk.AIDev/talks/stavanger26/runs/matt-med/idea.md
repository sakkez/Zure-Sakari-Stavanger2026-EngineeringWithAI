# Game idea

<!-- The same text goes to every run. It is vague on purpose: the product owner's
     brief.md holds the exact rules, the acceptance suite tests them, and only runs that
     ask the product-owner subagent get them. -->

A battle royale in the browser. Everyone who opens the page, on a phone or a laptop,
drops into the same arena with a generated name: a 30 by 30 grid seen from above.
You run around and hit the other players. The safe zone shrinks over time, and
outside it you get hurt. The last one standing wins, and then a new round starts.
If you're the only one there, a computer player shows up so you have someone to fight.
Kills, staying alive and winning all earn points, and there's a live highscore list.
You can see everyone's health, the damage you deal and take, when you can strike again,
and the rules.
It should look sharp and feel alive, in exciting, dynamic colours: smooth movement, hits
you can feel, a zone that visibly closes in.
If you die you can still watch. If you join in the middle of a round, you wait for the next one.
